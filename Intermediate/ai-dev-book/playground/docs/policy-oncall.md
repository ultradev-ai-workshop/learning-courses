# Policy: on-call

## Rotation
Each team runs a weekly on-call rotation that starts on Monday at 10:00. The pager must be acknowledged within 5 minutes for SEV1 and SEV2, and within 30 minutes for SEV3.

## Handover
The outgoing engineer writes a handover note listing open incidents and risky deploys. On-call engineers get one day off after a week with a night page.

## Escalation
If the primary on-call does not acknowledge in time, the pager escalates to the secondary, then to the engineering manager.
