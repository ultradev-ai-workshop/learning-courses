Chapter 14 · Utilities in Depth

Spacing & Sizing

Padding, margin, width and height are the utilities you'll type most. They all share the spacing scale from Chapter 3 (multiples of --spacing, 4 px by default), plus a few special values: fractions, full, screen, the dynamic viewport units, and named container sizes. This chapter covers the docs' Spacing and Sizing groups and the measurements behind them, checked in the browser.

You will learn

  • Padding and margin on every side, including logical sides and negative margins.
  • space-x/space-y versus gap, and mx-auto.
  • Widths and heights: the scale, fractions, full, fit, min, max, viewport units.
  • size-*, min-*/max-*, and the named container sizes like max-w-md.
  • Why h-dvh is usually better than h-screen on phones.

mx-auto centres a block that has a width: the 344 px left over (600 − 256) is split equally. Margins also accept negative values (-mt-4) to pull an element over the one before it, and m-px is exactly 1 px.

space-x / space-y vs gap

space-y-4 adds a margin between children: every child except the last gets a bottom margin. gap-4 does the same for flex and grid containers without touching the children:

💡 Prefer flex flex-col gap-4 or grid gap-4. space-* breaks when items wrap, when you hide a child with hidden (it still counts unless it's last), and when children set their own margins. gap has none of these problems.

🧠 Analogy: a box is a framed picture on a wall. Padding is the mat between the picture and the frame: it's inside, and the background colour fills it. Margin is the empty wall between frames: outside, always transparent. Gap is a rule the wall sets for all frames at once: "keep 16 px between each".

w-fit is as wide as the text inside; the others don't depend on content.

size-*: width and height together

For squares (avatars, icons) use size-10 instead of w-10 h-10. The tailwindcss canonicalize command (Chapter 4) suggests this.

Height and the viewport

Height utilities mirror width: h-12, h-1/2, h-full, h-fit. For "the whole screen" there are several units, because on phones the browser's address bar appears and disappears:

ClassCSSHeight
h-screen100vhClassic viewport height; on mobile it can be taller than what's visible.
h-dvh100dvhDynamic: changes as browser bars show and hide.
h-svh100svhSmall: with the bars visible (never hidden behind them).
h-lvh100lvhLarge: with the bars hidden.

For a full-page layout, min-h-dvh on the outer wrapper is the usual choice: at least the visible screen, and taller if the content needs it.

Min and max

min-w-*, max-w-*, min-h-*, max-h-* take the same values. The most common:

  • max-w-prose: 65 characters wide, a comfortable reading width.
  • max-w-md, max-w-2xl, max-w-7xl: named container widths (28 rem, 42 rem, 80 rem).
  • min-w-0: lets a flex or grid item shrink below its content (Chapter 13).
  • max-h-96 overflow-y-auto: a scrollable list of limited height.

Recap

  • p-/m- with x, y, t, r, b, l, s, e, bs, be; any number on the spacing scale; negative margins with -.
  • mx-auto centres a block with a width; prefer gap over space-*.
  • Widths: scale, fractions (w-1/3), full, fit, container sizes (w-md), viewport units.
  • size-* sets width and height together.
  • h-dvh/min-h-dvh fit mobile screens better than h-screen.
  • max-w-prose for reading; min-w-0 to let flex items shrink.

Exercises

Exercise 1 🟢 easy compute

What are px-5, mt-0.5 and w-60 in pixels?

Show solution

20 px left and right; 2 px; 240 px.

Exercise 2 🟢 easy write

Make a 48 px square, rounded, with 8 px of space to its right.

Show solution

size-12 rounded-md me-2 (or mr-2).

Exercise 3 🟢 easy reading width

A blog article is too wide on large screens. Limit it to a comfortable reading width and centre it.

Show solution

mx-auto max-w-prose (add px-4 so it doesn't touch the screen edges on phones).

Exercise 4 🟢 easy viewport

Which class makes a hero section exactly the visible screen height on a phone, adjusting as the address bar hides?

Show solution

h-dvh (or min-h-dvh if its content may be taller).

Exercise 5 🟡 medium space vs gap

A tag list uses flex flex-wrap space-x-2. When it wraps, the second line starts with an indent and there's no vertical space. Fix it.

Show solution

Replace space-x-2 with gap-2: gap applies between items in both directions and doesn't add margins that misalign wrapped lines.

Exercise 6 🟡 medium fit

A badge in a flex-col container stretches to full width. Make it only as wide as its text without changing the container.

Show solution

Add self-start (stop stretching) or w-fit to the badge.

Exercise 7 🟡 medium playground

In chapters/14/, wrap the page content in the centred container from "More examples". Measure its width at 800 px and 1400 px with DevTools and explain the numbers.

Show solution

At 800 px the container is 800 px wide (less than max-w-5xl's 1024 px). 800 is above sm but below lg (1024), so the padding is 24 px from sm:px-6. At 1400 px it's capped at 1024 px, centred with 188 px margins, and has 32 px padding.

Exercise 8 🟡 medium scroll

Make a notifications list at most 20 rem tall that scrolls when longer.

Show solution

max-h-80 overflow-y-auto (80 × 0.25 rem = 20 rem).

Exercise 9 🔴 hard logical

Your app supports Arabic (right-to-left). An icon has mr-2 to separate it from the text after it. What goes wrong in RTL, and which class fixes it?

Show solution

In RTL the text is on the icon's left, but mr-2 still adds space on the right, so the gap is on the wrong side. me-2 (margin-inline-end) adds space after the icon in the reading direction, whatever it is.

Exercise 10 🔴 hard explain

Why does space-y-4 generate :where(.space-y-4 > :not(:last-child)) with margins on both start and end, controlled by a --tw-space-y-reverse variable?

Show solution

The selector targets every child of .space-y-4 except the last. The two margins plus the reverse variable let the same utility work with flex-col-reverse: space-y-reverse sets the variable to 1, moving the margin from the end to the start. :where() keeps the specificity low so a child's own margin utility can still override it.
